Frost Radar

Protocols whose record carries no token yet, ranked by what a launch would be worth if the record stayed as it is — and what to do to farm each one before it issues one.

How the value is modelled

Implied value = TVL × 0.30 × growth multiplier × seasoning. The growth multiplier comes from the protocol’s seven-day TVL change, clamped to −75%…+200% and weighted 0.6, so a hot week lifts the value and a cold week cuts it, but neither decides it alone. Seasoning starts at 0.70 and saturates toward 1.00 as the protocol ages without a token (half-seasoned after about a year); a protocol with no tracking date is read as 180 days old, a deliberately young guess. Every row’s detail view shows its own three numbers.

A protocol “records no token” means its DefiLlama record carries none — not proof it has none. Tokens that already trade are listed separately, at the market’s own value, and never mixed into this ranking. This board is the entry’s own claim about value; it does not predict whether or when a token is issued.
