    How a launch is priced

    Implied value = TVL × market multiple × growth multiplier × chain breadth × seasoning. Growth comes from the seven-day TVL change (clamped to −75%…+200%, weighted 0.6); breadth is logarithmic in the chain count; seasoning rises from 0.70 toward 1.00 as the protocol ages without a token. A median, not a mean: mcap/TVL across live tokens runs from 0.0003 to 400, so one outlier must not set the price.

    Out-of-sample evidence

    The multiple is tested, not trusted: each live token is priced from the median of the other comparables, and the medians fitted on the older half of the dated tokens price the newer half. The miss is the factor between the priced value and the real market cap, so ×2 means half or double. The multiple is a rough anchor — which is why every implied value carries an honest range, the value divided and multiplied by the backtest’s median miss.

    The comparables, plotted

    Market cap against TVL on log scales: the 128 live tokens (grey) set the multiple; the board’s 198 protocols (teal) are priced from them; the line is the global median multiple, 0.21.

    Farm verdicts

    Each row’s verdict nets its farm plan against one stated scenario: a $1,000 deposit, 10% of the implied value paid to depositors, pro-rata by the deposit’s share of TVL. The slice is 10% × implied value × ($1,000 ÷ TVL); the net is the slice minus the plan’s gas bill, added up from the plan’s own steps. FARM means the net is at least $10, MARGINAL at least $0, THIN at least −$25, SKIP anything worse. The plan’s own minimum deposit is $50–$200, so a reader farming the minimum should scale the slice down; the verdict prices the $1,000 position it states.
