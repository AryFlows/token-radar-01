Frost Radar

Protocols whose record carries no token yet, ranked by what a launch would be worth if the record stayed as it is — and what to do to farm each one before it issues one.

How the value is modelled

Implied value = TVL × market multiple × growth multiplier × chain breadth × seasoning. The multiple is the median mcap/TVL of this snapshot’s token-trading protocols — per category where the category holds at least three live tokens, else the global median. Growth comes from the seven-day TVL change (clamped to −75%…+200%, weighted 0.6); breadth is logarithmic in the chain count; seasoning rises from 0.70 toward 1.00 as the protocol ages without a token. Each row’s detail view shows its own five numbers.

A protocol “records no token” means its DefiLlama record carries none. Tokens that already trade are listed separately, at the market’s own value, and never mixed into this ranking.
